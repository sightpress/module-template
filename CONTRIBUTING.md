# Contributing to the SightPress module template

Thank you for helping. Everyone who takes part agrees to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

This repository is the starting point for new modules, so a change here reaches every module made from it later.
Please keep it small and plain: the template should pass every check, have no example features and be easy to
understand.

- **Report a problem or an accessibility barrier:** [open an issue](https://github.com/sightpress/module-template/issues/new).
  Say what you did, what happened and what you expected, and for a barrier, your assistive technology and browser.
- **Security problems:** never in a public issue. Email <security@sightpress.org>.
- **Without GitHub:** email <contribute@sightpress.org>.
- **Code:** one change per pull request, with a [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) title.
  `composer check` must pass.

Most of SightPress lives elsewhere: see [core](https://github.com/sightpress/core),
[module-interface](https://github.com/sightpress/module-interface) and the
[documentation](https://github.com/sightpress/docs) for the other ways to help.
